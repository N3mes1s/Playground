### Worst decisions / lessons
1. **NVDA starter on 5/20 @ 223.47 was too early.** Entered on a single
   reversal candle off 220 after a slide from 236. Next two days = distribution
   (227→219 on 203M vol, then break of 220 support). The signal was a *bounce*,
   not a *base*. Lesson: for a single-name pullback entry in a corrective
   leg, wait for at least 2–3 days of tight range + reclaim of a moving
   average, not just one reversal bar.
2. **Did not trim NVDA on 5/21 distribution day.** Wrote in the journal "if
   breaks 217 tomorrow, trim half" but on 5/22 it closed 215.33 — broke 217 —
   and I held citing "no Friday adds." That rule is about *adding* risk, not
   *holding* already-broken theses. Should have trimmed half on the 220 break.
3. **No hard stop entered on NVDA, only a "mental" stop.** Mental stops have
   slippage built in (emotional, not mechanical). When initiating a single
   name with a thesis-defined invalidation, place a working stop or at least
   journal a specific trigger price and *honor it*.

### Sharpened heuristics (carry forward)
- **Pullback entries need confirmation, not just a reversal bar.** For an
  index ETF in a strong uptrend, one green day off support is enough. For a
  single name that's been distributing, demand a 2–3 day base or reclaim of
  the 10/20D MA.
- **Tranche cadence works.** Two tranches in SPY and QQQ avg'd in below the
  current price and limited single-day timing risk. Default plan: 1st tranche
  on signal, 2nd only on follow-through (next-day hold above entry or a
  deeper retest that holds support). Skip tranche 2 if signal invalidates.
- **Honor written stops mechanically.** If the journal says "trim if X breaks
  Y," execute on close that day. "Friday no-adds" ≠ "Friday no-trims." Friday
  is for *reducing* risk.
- **Cash is a position.** Sitting at 60% cash all week is fine when only
  one of three names is showing relative strength. Don't deploy to a target
  allocation; deploy to setups.

### Retired / demoted heuristics
- **"Buy leadership names on the first reversal day after a pullback."**
  Burned by it in NVDA. Replaced by the confirmation rule above. Single names
  ≠ indices.
- **"Mental stops are fine for starter-size positions."** Retire. Even a 4-
  share NVDA is real risk; either place a working stop or pre-commit a
  specific exit and execute on close.
