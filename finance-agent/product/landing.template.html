The trade-off

We won't beat the market in a rally.
We won't lose your shirt in a crash.

Most retail strategies fail in the same way: they get too aggressive at the top and panic-sell at the bottom. The agent inverts that. It sizes small, holds cash through chop, scouts only on confirmed reversals, and rewrites its own rules every Friday based on what worked.

In 2022's three-month sprint through a 50bp + 75bp + 75bp Fed hiking cycle — capped by an 8.6% then 9.1% CPI print — it lost 11 cents on $10,000. SPY lost $769.

How it works

Every weekday, the agent runs a tick.

01 · 9:35 ET
Macro/policy scan

Pulls overnight headlines: Trump/admin commentary on tickers, Fed speak, tariff news, executive orders, regulatory actions. Maps each to sector impact.

02 · throughout
Tape read

Quotes, OHLC history, regime tag (TREND-UP / RANGE / TREND-DOWN / CAPITULATION). Decides if the regime supports new risk.

03 · 15:55 ET
Order decision

Most days end with no trade. Orders carry a one-line written thesis, an invalidation level, and a Day-2 weakness criterion. Hard rails enforced in code.

04 · Friday close
Playbook rewrite

Reviews the week's trades and journal. Identifies what worked, what failed, why. Rewrites the playbook in full. Next Monday opens on the new rules.

What you get every Friday

The agent's own playbook. Verbatim.

Most "AI for trading" products give you a black box: signals, scores, alerts. We give you the thinking.

The playbook is the agent's working memory. After every weekly close, it reviews the journal and rewrites its own rules — sizing, stops, regime mapping, entry triggers. You see exactly how it's adapting.

If you're a self-directed trader, you get a second opinion from a system that journals everything and never tilts. If you're a passive investor, you get a read on whether to actually be deployed.
