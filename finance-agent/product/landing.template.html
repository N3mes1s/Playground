The thesis

We won't beat the market in a rally.
We won't lose your shirt in a crash.

Most retail strategies fail the same way: too aggressive at the top, panic-sell at the bottom. The agent inverts that. It sizes small, holds cash through chop, scouts only on confirmed reversals, and rewrites its own rules every Friday based on what worked.
